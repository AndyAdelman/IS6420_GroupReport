
Project purpose:
The project will model the customer-facing Domino’s online ordering process. The database will support selecting a store, browsing its menu, customizing products, submitting an order, making a payment, choosing pickup or delivery, and tracking order status.
The database will include only the store information needed to support online ordering and fulfillment. It will not model Domino’s broader corporate or store-management operations.

The process begins when a customer:
Enters an address or location.
Selects an available Domino’s store.
Chooses pickup or delivery.
Browses the store’s available menu.
Process ending

The process ends when:
Payment is recorded.
The order is fulfilled or cancelled.
The final order status is recorded.
Applicable loyalty activity is recorded.

Included:
Registered customers and guest orders
Customer addresses
Store selection
Store-specific menus
Menu categories and products
Product sizes and variations
Toppings, sauces, and other customizations
Orders and order items
Coupons and promotions
Payments and payment status
Pickup and delivery
Order-status history
Customer loyalty accounts and transactions
Synthetic sample data
Proposed pizza-subscription venture

Excluded:
Ingredient-level inventory
Suppliers and distribution centers
Employee scheduling and payroll
Franchise accounting
Detailed kitchen preparation
Driver assignment and GPS tracking
Corporate marketing systems
Payment-card storage and processor internals
Tax calculation rules
Customer-service case management

Assumptions:
Each order is fulfilled by one store.
An order may be associated with a registered customer or placed as a guest order.
A customer may maintain multiple addresses.
Available products, prices, and promotions may vary by store.
An order contains one or more order items.
Each order item represents a selected product variation and may have multiple customizations.
An order uses either pickup or delivery.
The system records payment results but does not store complete card information.
All project data will be synthetic.
